Their source is available at the referenced repositories. You may replace
these libraries with compatible modified versions and reverse engineer the
application for debugging modifications to these LGPL-covered libraries
as permitted by the LGPL. This preview does not impose an additional
restriction on those rights.

Build-time Wine and its temporary compatibility components are not included
in this folder. All redistributed DLL/PYD files are checked against the
Windows Python distribution or the installed pinned Windows wheels.
